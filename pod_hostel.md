## May 8th journal — capsule hotel description

> I am sleeping in a pod; a hostel on the other side of town has converted bunk-bed rooms into a capsule hotel, filling the room with interlocking plywood to create 10 pods per room. Each room has a toilet, a shower and two sinks. Two rooms share a small kitchen that reeks of ramen packets.
