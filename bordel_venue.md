## Conference notes — Municipal House background

> *Card description:* Used to be site of Royal Court palace. Demolished early 20th century. Constructed from 1905, opened 1912, designed by Osvald Polívka and Antonín Balšánek.
>
> Constructed in Art Nouveau style, with exterior stucco and allegorical art. Mosaic is Homage to Prague by Karel Špillar. Sculptures on either side represent "The Degradation of the People" and "The Resurrection of the People" by Ladislav Šaloun.

> *Aria:* "Smetana Hall" comes from the famous Czech composer Bedrich Smetana, recognised as the creator of the "modern" Czech Music (modern for the 1860s). He had died in 1884, 18 years before building work began.

> *Sylvia:* Art Nouveau, constructed in 1905, used to be site of Royal Court palace, also known as Smetana Hall, more information on trello.

> *Sylvia:* Mario: The problem is that, basically, all of the hackerspaces that I visited or found, the big bottleneck was that we are in someone else's land. Somebody owns us. We need to pay rent; we need to survive based on their terms.
>
> What if we actually own a place? What if we are able to get our own space that we can maybe pay a loan on and therefore make it more sustainable, make it completely ours? We can tear down walls. The way we did it is that we are crypto-native misfits. We like to do mischief, so we just made our own loan.
